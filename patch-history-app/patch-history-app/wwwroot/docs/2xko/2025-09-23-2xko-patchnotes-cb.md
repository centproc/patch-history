<h3 id="top">General Changes</h3>
<strong><a href="/patch-history/docs/2xko/" style="text-decoration: none;">back to patch list</a></strong>

<p>
We’ve made several bugfixes and backend improvements, and are still investigating a few bugs.

<i>Note: We’ll do our best to keep updates and any downtime around Tuesday 9am PT—so try to avoid scheduling any online tournaments during that time.</i>

<h3>Fixed Issues</h3>

* Fixed an issue causing high-ping matches.
* Fixed an issue with some of Vi’s abilities that caused the opponent to lose control of their character.
* Fixed an issue where Blitzcrank could perform their Ultimate for two bars by kara-canceling <i>Spinning Turbine</i> (→ <strong>S2</strong>).
* Fixed an issue where champions did not have throw invulnerability when entering after an Eject.
    * This throw invulnerability is lost when performing an attack.
* Fixed an issue where it was possible to soft lock by using save states.

<h3>Known Issues</h3>

* Certain peripherals constantly input ↑ and ←. If you’re experiencing this, refer to our <a href="https://support-2xko.riotgames.com/hc/en-us/articles/31895225783059-2XKO-Supported-Devices-Peripheral-Troubleshooting">support article</a> for possible workarounds. 
* Credits are incorrectly being shown as a reward after a private lobby match. Credits can only be earned in the Ranked Lobby and Casual Lobby. This visual bug will be fixed at a later time. 

<h3>Balance Adjustments</h3>

We’ll be shipping various balance adjustments in small updates for the next few months. We’re seeing healthy win rates across all levels of skill, which is great! But we’ve noticed a few places where champions either weren’t behaving as intended, or were breaking the speed limit in damage.

We’re not going to be changing core strategies in this patch—though Juggernaut Blitz players may have been hit. We want time for players to find adequate counterplay before nerfing strategies or significantly changing the meta. We’re keeping an eye on tournaments and ranked, and will make adjustments for the health of the meta when necessary. You have permission to cook.
</p>

<h2 id="blitzcrank">Blitzcrank</h2>

<br>
<p>

* <i>Rocket Grab</i> (<strong>S1</strong>) now causes two hits of damage scaling, up from zero.
* <i>Air Purifier</i> (↓ <strong>S1</strong>) now causes one hit of damage scaling, up from zero.
* Mid-air <i>Rocket Grab</i> (<strong>S1</strong>) now causes one hit of damage scaling, up from zero.
* Steam-empowered <i>Rocket Grab</i> (<strong>S1</strong>) now causes one hit of damage scaling, up from zero.
    * Previously, <i>Rocket Grab</i> had zero scaling until you performed a follow-up. Because of this, <i>Rocket Punch</i> (<strong>S2</strong>) or <i>Rocket Grab</i> confirms were converting into more damage than close-range confirms when using system mechanics (Fury Break, Double Down, etc.) or supers.
    * This change tones down Blitzcrank’s damage off of long-range conversions, while keeping their close-range damage high.
* <i>Helping Hand</i> (<strong>S1</strong> super) knockdown speed has been increased. It now has one less active frame, and the upper hitbox is slightly smaller.
    * This change prevents <strong>L</strong> from connecting after <i>Helping Hand</i> (<strong>S1</strong> super). We’re okay with creativity, but this was breaking our intended combo pathing in a way we didn’t find particularly interesting or balanced. This change also reduces the frame advantage of <i>Helping Hand</i> (<strong>S1</strong> super) by around two frames. 
    * We tested all of the Blitz Double Down TOD combos you’ve been posting, and we’re sorry to say they still work just fine.
* <i>Helping Hand</i> (<strong>S1</strong> super) no longer breaks armor.
* <i>Spinning Turbine’s</i> (→ <strong>S2</strong>) second and third hit now leaves opponents slightly closer on block. All hits will connect with a blocking opponent in most situations.
* Increased air recovery of mid-air <strong>L</strong> from 8f to 17f.
* Increased air recovery of mid-air <strong>M</strong> from 10f to 24f.
    * Blitzcrank having low recovery on air normals was unintended. 
    * This brings Blitzcrank more in line with other champs’ air normal recovery.

</p>

<h2 id="illaoi">Illaoi</h2>

<br>
<p>

* <strong>S2</strong> super tentacles deal 33% less chip damage.
    * <strong>S2</strong> super tentacles were causing egregious chip setups, especially while in Fury or when the opponent was affected by Darius’s Bleed. 
    * This tones down their overall power level, but using them is still a viable strategy.
</p>

<h2 id="vi">Vi</h2>

<br>
<p>

* Vi now falls faster after a successful air throw, and it knocks opponents back less.
    * There were some heights where it was impossible for Vi to follow up with <strong>S2</strong> Super after air throw. We’re not opposed to this type of nuance, but not for Vi. 
    * Vi falls faster and the opponent is left closer to her to ensure ↓ <strong>S2</strong> is always a possible follow-up.
</p>

<h2 id="yasuo">Yasuo</h2>

<br>
<p>

* <i>Gale Blade</i> (mid-air <strong>S1</strong>) does 90 damage, down from 100.
* <i>Sweeping Blade</i> (→ <strong>S1</strong>) does 110 damage, down from 120.
* <i>Turbulent Wind</i> (mid-air <strong>S2</strong>) does 90 damage, down from 110.
    * Yasuo was doing far too much damage when players started to optimize his routes, outdamaging our big bodies (not accounting for Fuses). 
    * These numbers adjust his damage down to more reasonable levels.
</p>
