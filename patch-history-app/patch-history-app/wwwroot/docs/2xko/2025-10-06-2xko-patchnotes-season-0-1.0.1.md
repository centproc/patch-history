## System Changes
We're happy with how Closed Beta went, but we're excited to keep making 2XKO better. We feel like there are some outliers in the meta, and we want to use this patch to try to address those. From a systems side, we're looking to decrease the power of Double Down, Super Tag (Handshake Tagging during a Double Down Super), and the amount of meter gain. We're also looking to get Jinx, Ekko, and Yasuo more in line with the rest of the roster's power level, improving Braum and Blitzcrank, and making Ahri and Vi's combos more consistent. We're happy to see everyone playing 2XKO to its fullest potential. Keep cookin' y'all!

### Ranked
Ranked starts on October 9 with the release of patch 1.0.1.1! We want to make sure we include all of our 1.0.1.1 adjustments and back-end improvements from Closed Beta before we flip the switch.

Once Ranked is live, you should notice a few matchmaking adjustments thanks to the data we gathered from Closed Beta. The average queue time has increased to find more balanced matches. In general, matches should feel more fair, and placement games should get you closer to your expected rank. As a result, reaching Challenger will be more difficult and should require a bit more of a time investment.

We're still keeping an eye on Ranked matchmaking as we transition into Season 0, and will continue to improve the Ranked experience in future updates.
</p>

<p>

### Core Gameplay
* You can now take actions in mid-air after blocking attacks.
    * Previously, blocking an attack mid-air locked you out of performing air actions, even after air block stun ended. This tunes down the power level of moves like Vi's Crater Maker (↓ <strong>S2</strong>) and Blitzcrank's mid-air Garbage Collection (↓ <strong>S2</strong>).
    * All moves across all champions have increased or decreased air block stun on attacks to accommodate this change.
* Throw invulnerability while mid-air has been increased from 5 frames to 8 frames after blocking an attack.
    * All champions have an 8 frame or faster air <strong>L</strong>, which means all champions can now perform mid-air actions to beat air throws after blocking.
* Opponents recover from being hit by Break 10 frames earlier.
    * Break was feeling a bit too advantageous for the person spending their Break meter.
* Air hurtboxes and air collision is more consistent in any animation where a champion spins through the air.
    * This change has had a slight impact on advanced combos. Most combos still work as they did before, but there may be slight combo adjustments you have to make if you're the sweatiest of the sweats.
    * Note: this will set us up for success in the future, and Season 0 is the best time to make this change. Yasuo and Ekko were most affected, so we've tuned some data to mitigate the difference in their combos.
* Retreating Guard can no longer be performed by holding ↓←. It can only be performed by holding ←.
    * There was an option select that could be performed by holding ↓← and mashing the dash button. This wasn't causing any issues yet, but it reduced parity between using the dash button vs. using <strong>L</strong>+<strong>M</strong> to dash-so we wanted to address this before it could become a problem.
* Assist cooldown timer now starts as soon as the Assist champion leaves the screen.
    * Previously, there was a 30-frame gap between when an Assist champion left the screen and when the Assist timer started ticking down.
    * We've reduced this time to be near-instant, so you can use Tag Launcher as soon as your Assist champ is visually offscreen.
* Assists will now be available more often.
* <strong>Bug fixes</strong>:
    * After getting hit by Break or getup attack, you can now perform actions.
        * Previously, you were unable to perform parry, <strong>L</strong> , or jump after Break, and if the opponent performed a meaty you had no choice but to block. This bug was severely increasing the power of Break.
    * Double Down damage scaling now properly applies to Jinx's fireworks super P'Choo Bang Pew Pew Boom Boom (<strong>S1</strong> Super) and Illaoi's Wrath of Nagakabourous (<strong>S2</strong> Super) tentacles.
    * Fixed issue where running jumps could disable inputs on landing in very rare circumstances.
    * The first Quick Tag of a match no longer allows you to block during the Quick Tag animation.
    * Fixed issue where performing some supers would cause projectiles to disappear.

<strong>Note:</strong> Even if we didn't address feedback that you've raised in this patch, it's probably on our radar. We see the posts, but right now we're focused on fine tuning our core systems and balancing our roster's power level. We're here for the long haul-so we'll eventually address those other things, even if addressing them means choosing to leave them as-is in the future.

## 1.0.1.1 (Live Oct 9)

* Break is less advantageous when not performing a Handshake Tag.
    * Break advantage on Point champion on the ground is now +6.
    * Break advantage while mid-air can be anywhere from +0 to +11 depending on height.
* Meter gain has been reduced overall.
    * Meter gain is now decreased as a combo gets longer.
        * At 8 seconds, meter gain is 80% of normal value.
        * At 11 seconds, meter gain is 50% of normal value.
        * At 16 seconds, meter gain is 10% of normal value.
    * Spending meter now decreases meter gain by 75% for 8 seconds.
        * This penalty goes away once the opponent recovers from a combo.
        * Since parry costs a bar of meter and refunds on success, this change reduces the potency of combos performed after parry.
    * Limit Strike bonus meter gain has been reduced from 40 to 25.
        * We're seeing healthy levels of meter at most levels of play, but at extremely advanced levels there was far too much meter being generated. We're adding penalties to meter gain on advanced combos to address this and encourage combo decisionmaking.
* Handshake Tag no longer prevents gray health from being accumulated.
    * This was a mostly unknown trait, but the strongest option in neutral was to Assist ↘ Handshake Tag, or to Super ↘ Handshake Tag. Previously, any damage dealt after these hits was unrecoverable, which made it so these hits were disproportionately rewarding. This is no longer the case.

### Fuses

* Juggernaut: Break meter on round start has been reduced from 100% to 75%.
    * Juggernaut starting each round with a full bar of Break led to round-start death situations. Juggernaut's Break meter also no longer resets between rounds, bringing it in line with other Fuses.
* Double Down: Performing a Double Down Super results in the second super dealing less damage.
    * Damage penalty was increased from 20% to 30%.
* Sidekick: Performing a Sidekick Super now results in the second super dealing less damage.
    * Damage penalty was increased from 20% to 30%.
* Double Down and 2X Assist: the cooldown on using Assists after activating Double Down or 2X Assist has been increased to 4 seconds.
    * Previously, activating Double Down or 2X Assist put Assists on the normal cooldown, which is 2 seconds.
    * Freestyle has an increased Assist cooldown when activated, so we're bringing 2X Assist and Double Down in-line.
* Sidekick: you can no longer perform more than one Double Down in a single combo.
    * We're bringing Sidekick in line with Double Down, and removing the ability to Sidekick Super multiple times per combo.

### Tutorials

* Tutorial UI, flow, and menus have been updated.
* The First Fight tutorial has received some visual updates.
* You'll now receive a set of avatar items for completing tutorials.
</p>

<h2 id="ahri">Ahri</h2>

<br>

<p>
We’re buffing Ahri significantly by improving her air combo stability and ground combo stability. We’re also making her <i>Spirit Rush</i> (any direction + <strong>S2</strong>) routes more stable in Happy Birthday situations and against cornered opponents. To balance this out, we’re dialing back some of her neutral tools. We believe that with these changes, Ahri will be overall stronger and perform more consistently.
</p>

<p>

* Dialing back Ahri’s neutral tools 
    * <i>Spirit Rush</i> (any direction + <strong>S2</strong>) can no longer be performed into another air Spirit Rush if the first Spirit Rush doesn’t connect.
       * Ahri was able to run away and turn into a hitbox, which was annoying at mid levels of play. This slows down her ability to suspend mid-air for extended amounts of time, while preserving her combo routes.
    * Standing <strong>M</strong> startup has been increased by 1 frame. It was 9 frames and is now 10 frames.
        * We’re increasing the startup to move her power to the parts of her kit that make her special. 
    * <i>Spirit Rush Assist</i> (→ <strong>T</strong>) hurtbox size has been increased. It now extends in front of Ahri.
* Small adjustments
    * <i>Hyper Spirit Rush</i> (<strong>S2</strong> Super) damage has been reduced by 9. It was 272 damage and is now 263.
    * Standing <strong>H</strong> block stun has been reduced by 1.
        * <strong>H</strong> into <i>Wild Flares</i> (<strong>S2</strong>) is still a true blockstring.
</p>

### 1.0.1.1 (Live Oct 9)

<p>

Ahri’s combos were dropping in a lot of unexpected ways. The changes below should address that.

* Ground combo consistency improvements
    * Standing  and crouching  height has been increased, and distance it pushes the opponent has been decreased.
    * Standing  height has been increased.
    * Mid-air  pushes opponent less on hit.
    * <i>Spirit Rush</i> (→ <strong>S2</strong>) combos more consistently in a Happy Birthday.
    * <strong>Bug fix:</strong> <i>Spirit Rush</i> (→ <strong>S2</strong>) ↘ <i>Spirit Rush</i> (up-forward <strong>S2</strong>) now properly combos against cornered opponents from max range.
* Air combo consistency improvements
    * Crouching <strong>H</strong> launch height vs. airborne opponents has been adjusted to align with the rest of the roster.
    * Mid-air charged <strong>H</strong> hit stun has been increased, and launch angle has been adjusted to keep the opponent closer to Ahri.
    * Mid-air <i>Foxfire Blast</i> (<strong>S1</strong>) hit stun has been slightly increased.
    * ↑→ <i>Spirit Rush</i> (↑→ <strong>S2</strong>) launch value has been slightly adjusted.
    * Mid-air <i>Spirit Rush</i> (any direction <strong>S2</strong>) knockback has been adjusted to keep opponents slightly closer.
    * ↓→ <i>Spirit Rush</i> (↓→ <strong>S2</strong>) travel distance has been increased, and it now combos more reliably from mid-air attacks.
* Quality of life buffs
    * <i>Foxfire Rain Assist</i> (← <strong>T</strong>) block stun has been increased.
    * <i>Charm</i> (Ultimate) projectile hitbox size has been increased.
</p>

<h2 id="blitzcrank">Blitzcrank</h2>

<br>

<p>
In the last balance patch, we reduced Blitzcrank’s damage on long range grabs. We want Blitzcrank players to be rewarded for getting close to opponents, so we’re improving their Steam-empowered command grab and kara command grab. We’re also giving Blitzcrank a few quality of life buffs.
</p>

<p>

* Adjustments
    * Air command grab can no longer be performed while extremely low to the ground.
    * The core rule changes also removed Blitzcrank’s ability to true blockstring aerial opponents into a command grab. We’re okay with chasing air tech with throw—go ahead, that’s fun and we want you to do it.
        * Blocking in the air and being guaranteed to be thrown afterward would eventually lead to extremely unhealthy play.
        * Blitzcrank still has a major advantage when you block their mid-air attacks, but an air command grab is no longer guaranteed.
* Blitzcrank bug fixes
    * Fury dash cancel can now be performed at the end of ← throw. 
    * Steam no longer decays during screen freeze (like during Handshake Tag and Ultimates).
    * <i>Garbage Collection</i> ↓ <strong>S2</strong> no longer grants extra meter on counterhit.
    * Helping Hand (<strong>S1</strong> Super) no longer grants Blitzcrank steam when using Sidekick Fuse.
    * Mid-air ← throw now deals 210 damage (matches mid-air → throw).
</p>

### 1.0.1.1 (Live Oct 9)

<p>

* Rewarding getting in close
    * Kara command grab distance has been increased slightly. This should make it easier to land when you correctly anticipate your opponent will be performing a retreating guard.
    * Steam-empowered <i>Garbage Collection</i> ↓ <strong>S2</strong> and mid-air ↓ <strong>S2</strong> now leaves opponents closer, which allows Blitzcrank to combo into Static Field (<strong>S2</strong> Super).
* Quality of life buffs
    * <i>Rocket Grab</i> (<strong>S1</strong>) and <i>Air Purifier</i> (↓ <strong>S1</strong>) followups now come out when held or when pressed.
    * When <i>Rocket Grab</i> (<strong>S1</strong>), <i>Air Purifier</i> (↓ <strong>S1</strong>), and mid-air <i>Rocket Grab</i> hit an Assist champion, they now have more hit stun. This also applies when Blitzcrank is tagged out before they connect.
        * This situation rarely came up, but when it did, it led to a bad outcome for Blitzcrank. Blitzcrank now has more of an advantage when this situation occurs, and often scores a soft knockdown.
* Blitzcrank bug fixes
    * Mid-air <i>Rocket Grab</i> (<strong>S1</strong>) followup no longer makes opponents enter on the wrong side when a champion is KO’d during the animation.
    * <i>Helping Hand</i> (<strong>S1</strong> Super) the spinning arm no longer has a hurtbox.
        * This is to prevent Blitzcrank from getting hit during super animation. (Blitzcrank still has a hurtbox on this move, but will no longer be grabbing <i>Timewinders</i> out of the air from behind them.)
    * Fixed bugs around <i>Rocket Grab</i> (<strong>S1</strong>).
    * Mid-air <i>Rocket Grab</i> (<strong>S1</strong>) will no longer occasionally fail to pull in an enemy.
    * <i>Rocket Grab</i> (<strong>S1</strong>) will be more consistent in Happy Birthdays.

<h2 id="ekko">Ekko</h2>

<br>

<p>
We think Ekko is strong, and we want to tone him down to be more on par with the rest of the roster. In this patch, we’re keeping his offensive mixup potential, but we’re taking power away from <i>Timewinder</i> (<strong>S1</strong>).

<i>Timewinder</i> is now riskier to use, which should preserve an aggressive playstyle and encourage Ekko players to set Afterimages in neutral. We’ll continue to keep an eye on Ekko’s offensive capabilities.
</p>

<p>

* Assist nerfs
    * <i>Chronostrike Assist</i> (→ <strong>T</strong>) hurtbox size has been increased. It now extends in front of Ekko. 
    * <i>Chronostrike Assist</i> (→ <strong>T</strong>) hitbox size has been slightly reduced to match <i>Chronostrike</i> (<strong>S2</strong>).
* Combo adjustments
    * During mid-air charged <i>Chronostrike</i> (<strong>S2</strong>), pushback applied to Ekko in the corner on hit and block has been reduced.

<h2 id="jinx">Jinx</h2>

<br>

<p>
<i>Zaaaapp!</i> (<strong>S2</strong> Super) ↘ Handshake Tag while in Double Down was dominating Jinx’s kit. We don’t want to eliminate this as a strategy entirely, because we think it can have a place in the game, but <i>Zaaaapp!</i> was too easy to access and far too rewarding on hit. In addition to the system changes above, we’re lowering <i>Zaaaapp!</i> damage, and combos following <i>Zaaaapp!</i> will be shorter. We hope these adjustments will encourage players to explore other strategies. We’ll be keeping an eye on Jinx; we believe if she decides to skip neutral, there should be a real cost in doing so.
</p>

<p>

* Quality of life buffs
    * <i>Pow Pow P-Pow!</i> (<strong>S1</strong>) can now be canceled into <i>Fishbones!</i> (<strong>S2</strong>) at any point during the sequence.
        * <i>Pow Pow P-Pow!</i> often fed the opponent a large amount of meter if they knew how to parry. This change allows Jinx to mix up her timing, lets her shoot <i>Flame Chompers!</i> (→ <strong>S1</strong>) more effectively, and helps with confirming this move on airborne opponents.
    * Mid-air <i>Fishbones, Hop!</i> (↓ <strong>S2</strong>) and mid-air <i>Fishbones!</i> (<strong>S2</strong>) momentum applied to Jinx was adjusted slightly.
    * Mid-air <i>Fishbones, Hop!</i> (↓ <strong>S2</strong>) no longer launches opponents as high
        * Both changes to mid-air <i>Fishbones, Hop!</i> (↓ <strong>S2</strong>) and mid-air <i>Fishbones!</i> (<strong>S2</strong>) enables fun new combos.
* Nerfs
    * <i>P'Choo Bang Pew Pew Boom Boom</i> (<strong>S1</strong>) damage has been reduced by 20% on average.
        *This Super was doing far too much damage, being the highest damage Super in the game. This change brings her damage down to more reasonable levels.
    * <i>Flame Chompers!</i> (→ <strong>S1</strong>) block stun on first hit has been increased.
        * Jinx can no longer throw the opponent between hits.
* Jinx bug fixes
    * <i>P'Choo Bang Pew Pew Boom Boom</i> (<strong>S1</strong> Super) corner hit push caused by projectiles has been removed.
    * <i>Zap!</i> (← <strong>S2</strong>) hitbox’s lower portion has been increased.
        * <i>Zap!</i> will now properly hit a crouching Ekko, ma meilleure ennemie.
    * Fixed issue where <i>Flame Chompers!</i> (→ <strong>S1</strong>)  would loop audio in Training mode when repeatedly resetting.

<h2 id="yasuo">Yasuo</h2>

<br>

<p>
We recently reduced Yasuo’s damage to be more in line with other champions. We’re happy with the results, and his win rate remains strong. However, Yasuo was still cheating the system in ways that other champions could not. We’re adding recovery to some moves that were too difficult to punish when Yasuo misses his opponent. Yasuo will have to place his shots more carefully if he wants to hold down the neutral.
</p>

<p>

* Hurtbox adjustments
    * <strong>L</strong> now has a custom hurtbox.
    * ↓ <strong>L</strong> now has a custom hurtbox.
        * Yasuo ↓ <strong>L</strong> was going underneath attacks unintentionally. Yasuo’s crouching jab is quite strong, and we don’t want him to be low profiling with this.
* Combo adjustments
    * Mid-air ↓ <strong>H</strong> hit stun has been decreased late into combos.
        * Yasuo’s combo length was a bit long for our tastes. Mid-air ↓<strong>H</strong>↘ mid-air <strong>S1</strong> or mid-air <strong>S2</strong> is no longer guaranteed, which helps shorten it.
    * <i>Turbulent Wind</i> (<strong>S2</strong>) and mid-air <i>Turbulent Wind</i> (<strong>S2</strong>) are now active for 2 more frames, and frames between each hit increased from 2 frames to 4 frames.
        * The air collision adjustments made some of Yasuo’s advanced combos drop. To fix this, we’ve adjusted Turbulent Wind to juggle the opponents for slightly longer if spaced properly.
        * Note: We don’t like changing move behavior this dramatically—however, combo expression is a core part of Yasuo’s kit. We want to keep Turbulent Wind combos, so this change allows most of Yasuo’s advanced combos to still function properly while opening up some new routes.
* Nerfs
    * <i>Wind Wall</i> (← <strong>S2</strong>) recovery has now been increased by 4 frames. It was 29 frames and is now 33 frames.
        *We're slightly increasing the recovery of Wind Wall to nudge down Yasuo’s zoning.
* Yasuo bug fixes
    * <i>Gale Blade</i> (<strong>S1</strong>) ↘ <i>Returning Gale</i> (<strong>S1</strong>) ↘ <i>Settling Gale</i> (<strong>S2</strong>) followup timings are now consistent. It was 26 frames then 30 frames, but is now 25 frames across the sequence.
    * <strong>L</strong> ↘ <strong>L</strong> no longer forces a <strong>L</strong>. 
    * <i>Settling Gale</i> (mid-air <strong>S1</strong> ↘ <strong>S2</strong>) hitbox is no longer active during recovery frames.
    * ↓ <strong>H</strong> second hitbox is now the proper size when making contact with the opponent.
        * Note: When performing moves that create multiple hitboxes over multiple frames, we spawn all hitboxes at once on contact to make Happy Birthdays stable —you can notice this on moves like <i>Darius Cleave</i> (↓ <strong>H</strong>). The Happy Birthday magic hitbox on Yasuo <strong>H</strong> was the wrong size. It’s the right size now.
    * <i>Turbulent Wind</i> (<strong>S2</strong>) no longer teleports Yasuo when canceling mid-air into a Handshake Tag while in Freestyle.
        * Thanks for not finding this one.
    * <i>Gale Blade</i> (<strong>S1</strong>) and <i>Returning Gale</i> (↘ <strong>S1</strong>) now regain collision when they miss.
</p>

### 1.0.1.1 (Live Oct 9)

<p>

* Health has been reduced from 1000 to 975.
* Frame data nerfs
    * <strong>L</strong> and <strong>L</strong> ↘ <strong>L</strong> recovery increased by 2 frames. It was +1 on block and is now -1.
    * ↓ <strong>L</strong> recovery increased by 2 frames. It was +0 on block and is now -2.
        * <strong>L</strong> attacks that are 6 frames startup should not be plus without a severe drawback. Given the strength of Yasuo’s <strong>L</strong> attacks, we’re reducing the frame advantage by 2 to be more in line with the rest of the cast.
    * <strong>M</strong> recovery has been increased by 2 frames. It was 16 frames and is now 18 frames. 
    * Mid-air ↓ <strong>M</strong> recovery has been increased by 6 frames. It was 24 frames and is now 30 frames.
    * <i>Steel Tempest</i>  (<strong>S1</strong>) recovery has been increased by 8 frames. It was 21 frames and is now 29 frames.
        * Both <i>Steel Tempest</i>  (<strong>S1</strong>) and mid-air ↓ <strong>H</strong>  were usable without much consequence. We’re giving these moves the recovery they deserve to ensure Yasuo has to pick his shots carefully.
    * Mid-air <strong>L</strong> startup has increased by 1 frame. It was 6 frames and is now 7 frames.
        * Yasuo had the fastest aerial attack in the game at 6 frames. Yasuo should not have better frame data than other champions when air teching and pressing buttons, so we’re bringing this down to a more standard startup on a mid-air <strong>L</strong>.
    * <i>Settling Gale</i> (<strong>S1</strong> ↘ <strong>S2</strong>) has been changed from +2 on block to -7 on block.
        * Yasuo should not be plus when this happens—our bad.
* Hitbox and hurtbox adjustments
    * Mid-air charged <i>Steel Tempest</i> (<strong>S1</strong>) hitbox has been shortened in the front.
        * Your combos will still work, but performing this move in neutral should now win less often.
    * <i>Charged Sweeping Blade</i> (→ <strong>S1</strong>) can now be thrown while Yasuo is crossing up the opponent.
    * <i>Sweeping Blade Assist</i> (→ <strong>T</strong>) now has a hurtbox that extends in front of Yasuo.
    * <i>Lashing Wind</i> (<i>Calm</i> ↘ <strong>M</strong>) sword swipe now has a hurtbox.
    * <i>Lashing Wind</i> (<i>Calm</i> ↘ <strong>M</strong>) now launches opponents farther away when they block mid-air.
